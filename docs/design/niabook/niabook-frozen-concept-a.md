# NiaBook — Frozen direction (Concept A) + the cost-of-migration thesis

**Status: FROZEN as the board-demo direction.** Three Pockets is archived as a *lens*
that lives inside NiaBook, not as the product. This document supersedes the two workshop
files for build/demo purposes; they remain as the exploration record.

Design-only (Product Polish Phase). This spec is for a board demo, not a finished app.
The goal is to make NiaBook feel inevitable in the first minute. Same June scenario.

---

## 0 · The strategic breakthrough (why this matters beyond UI)

Three Pockets was a layout we set aside, but it exposed the real product truth:

> **Nia does not increase a worker's salary. Nia reduces the cost of migration.**

Without Nia, a migrant's salary is eaten by the cost of being away — rent, food,
transport, what he overpays at the market — and "whatever is left" reaches home or stays
his. With Nia, the *cost of being here shrinks*, so more money remains, so more reaches
home and more stays his.

```
Without Nia                     With Nia
  Salary                          Salary
    ↓                               ↓
  Rent · Food · Transport         Cost of being here goes down
  Utilities · Market prices         ↓
    ↓                             More money remains
  Whatever is left                  ↓
                                  More reaches home · more stays yours
```

Every rupee Nia saves is a rupee that **moves from "the cost of being here" into "money
that stays with you."** That single sentence is the business — cleaner than "continuity
infrastructure" — and NiaBook is where the Member *sees it happen* every month. This
thesis is carried into Concept A as strategy and as one story section. The Three Pockets
*layout* is not carried.

---

## 1 · What NiaBook is (unchanged)

The category-defining artefact: a **Book of Months.** Each month is a page; each page
opens with a **verdict** and tells the **story** of whether leaving home was worthwhile.
The pages accumulate — that is the progress the product records. Not a wallet, statement,
passbook, tracker, or dashboard. "Open your NiaBook."

---

## 2 · The one narrative

Everything on the page serves a single sentence: **migration creates value.** The page
proves it — earned, reached home, stayed yours, and the cost that Nia is working to
shrink. The Three Pockets insight *strengthens* this story (it names where the salary
went and shows Nia moving money toward the Member); it does not replace it.

---

## 3 · The page, top to bottom

1. **Page header** — "June 2026." The month is the page.
2. **The verdict** — "June was worth it." (state-dependent, never shame.)
3. **The hero** — money that became the Member's and his family's. Two candidate copies
   under test — see §5.
4. **The salary line** — "Your ₹14,000 salary arrived, in full and on time." Grounds the
   story: the money is real.
5. **Where your ₹14,000 went** — the Three-Pockets lens as *one section*: reached home,
   stayed yours, the cost of being here. Home and Yours in the received tone (good); the
   cost line neutral, framed as the toll Nia is working to lower — not a loss.
6. **What Nia made smaller** — the thesis made visible. "Nia kept ₹185 in your pocket at
   Sukh Store — money that would have gone to the market." Plus "₹500 voucher waiting"
   in grey. This is the strategy, on the page, without marketing.
7. **Progress** — "You kept ₹300 more than in May," and reachable earlier pages.

The difference from the old A: section 5 now explicitly names the three destinations
(the lens), and section 6 is reframed from "what Nia added" to **"what Nia made
smaller"** — the cost-reduction thesis rather than a generic bonus.

---

## 4 · The razor — "does this help explain whether leaving home was worthwhile?"

Applied to every element. Keep / demote / cut.

| Element | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Verdict line | **Keep** | It *is* the answer. |
| Hero (money yours + family's) | **Keep** | The proof, in one figure/pair. |
| Salary arrived | **Keep** | The money is real, in full, on time. |
| Reached home | **Keep** | The purpose of leaving. |
| Stayed with you | **Keep** | What he is building. |
| Cost of being here | **Keep, reframed** | Honesty *and* the setup for Nia's value — this is what Nia shrinks. |
| In your hand now | **Demote** | Useful (what's spendable) but it's utility, not the worth story. Quiet sub-line, muted. |
| What Nia made smaller | **Keep, promote** | The thesis made visible; the reason to use more Nia. |
| Voucher waiting | **Keep** | The flywheel; desire, held in grey. |
| Progress line | **Keep** | The "Book of Months" progress — the point of the name. |
| See earlier months | **Keep** | The book has pages. |
| Balances, dates, txn ids, charts | **Cut** | None answer the question; all make it a bank. |

Net change from the razor: **"in your hand now" is demoted** to a quiet sub-line under
"stayed yours," not a story beat.

## 5 · Hero copy — the test you asked for

The concern: "₹9,800 became yours and your family's" may be too abstract — the board may
get it, a Member may not.

- **Option 1 (the sum):** `₹9,800` · `became yours and your family's`
- **Option 2 (two concrete lines):** `₹5,000 reached home.` · `₹4,800 stayed with you.`

**My recommendation:** lead with **Option 2**. It is more instantly human — two familiar
destinations, no arithmetic, no abstraction — and the board is judging whether *Members*
will understand, so the more visceral line is the safer demo, not the riskier one. Keep
the ₹9,800 sum as a quiet total beneath ("₹9,800 of your ₹14,000 became yours and your
family's") so the aggregate is still there for the board without being the thing a Member
must decode.

Per your steer, the demo keeps the sum available; I'd put Option 2 on top and the sum
underneath, and let the room react. Both are rendered in the hi-fi so you can choose live.

## 6 · The five states (unchanged, in the "what Nia made smaller" band)

1. **Sukh Store savings** — "Nia kept ₹185 in your pocket at Sukh Store."
2. **Voucher unused** — "₹500 Sukh Store voucher waiting for you." (grey)
3. **Voucher redeemed** — "You used your ₹500 voucher — it stayed in your pocket."
4. **No shopping savings** — "Shop at Sukh Store and Nia keeps more in your pocket."
5. **No work through Nia** — "Work through Nia to unlock your ₹500 voucher." The verdict
   and the earned-money story still hold; only the Nia-made-smaller band is thin, which
   is itself the pull.

---

## 7 · Board demo choreography (first-minute goal)

What the board sees, in order:

1. The app opens **on NiaBook** — it is the first screen, not a tab you find. There is no
   "Wallet" anywhere; the concept is gone.
2. The June page opens **on the verdict** — "June was worth it" — before any number.
3. The story reads top to bottom in ten seconds: reached home, stayed yours, the cost
   Nia is shrinking, what Nia kept in his pocket.
4. A quick pass through the **five states** shows the flywheel: the grey voucher becomes
   the reason to work through Nia; savings prove the cost-reduction thesis.
5. One flip back to an earlier, thinner page shows **the book filling** — progress.

The board should leave the first minute able to say the thesis themselves: *Nia lowers
the cost of migration, and here is the proof, every month.*

---

## 8 · Guardrails — what we are NOT building

- **Not** Three Pockets as the organizing model. It is one section (a lens), nothing more.
- **Not** the finished app. Secondary tabs may stay incomplete if they don't hurt the
  demo. Do not overbuild secondary flows.
- **Not** any new backend, session, or contract work (Product Polish Phase holds).
- **Colour:** keep the single restrained received tone — it measurably helps the
  5-second read for a low-literacy Member (the one place colour-as-state earns its
  place). Everything else is ink and grey. If the board finds it fintech-y, the fallback
  to mono-blue is one token change.
- **Build vs prototype:** the demo artefact is a high-fidelity interactive prototype
  (fast to iterate on copy in the room). Wiring NiaBook as the live first screen in the
  Flutter app — home index → NiaBook, remove the Wallet concept, drop the verdict page in
  — is a small, separate step, ready on your word. I did not start it, per the freeze.
